# Domain Vocabulary

- **Titik**: Indonesian word for "Dot" or "Point", representing an instantaneous focal point for macOS productivity.
- **Liquid Glass**: Multi-layered refractive HUD substrate combining `NSVisualEffectView(.popover)`, 3-stop dispersion gradient, top specular glare arc, chamfered bevels, and dual-layer floating shadows.
- **SF Trio**: Apple's native San Francisco typography triad (`SF Pro`, `SF Pro Rounded`, `SF Mono`) powering the centralized typography architecture.
- **Selection Pill**: A spring-animated rounded rectangle gliding vertically to highlight the currently focused search result.
- **Dynamic Plugin**: A `.dylib` shared library exposing `titik_plugin_entry` conforming to `plugin_api.h`.
